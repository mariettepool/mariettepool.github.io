Everything StickyPaper does

One planner, built around how a real family's day actually runs.

👪

Family home page

A shared family to-do list, today's family events, tonight's dinner, the grocery list, kids' chores, and earning-chore rewards — all in one place everyone can see.

🧹

Chore lists that repeat

Assign chores to each kid with flexible repeat options — daily, every 2 days, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, or weekdays only — with a progress bar so everyone can see how the day's chores are going.

💰

Bonus chores & rewards

Add extra, optional tasks any kid can claim to earn a reward — money, screen time, a playdate, a family outing, or whatever you set. Parents approve once it's done.

🏠

Everyone's own Today page

Personal to-do list, daily focus, water tracker, your own calendar events, and any lists just for you. Kids get a simplified view with their own chores, to-do list, and events.

📅

Real, editable calendars

Connect your own personal calendar and/or one shared family calendar. Add, edit, and delete real events, and colour-code them by category — birthdays, work, social, and more.

🍽

Meals & recipes

Plan dinners, manage a shared grocery list, and keep your favourite recipes — title, ingredients, method, and a link back to where you found it.

📋

Flexible lists

Create tick lists, weekly grids, or open notes — and choose whether each one shows on your own page or the whole family's.

👨‍👩‍👧

One planner, multiple sign-ins

Both parents can sign in separately — with Google or any email — and share the exact same family planner using a family code.
