Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how to cancel your StickyPaper subscription and when you can get your money back. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

Free trial

Every new account gets a 7-day free trial and nothing is charged during it. You are only billed if you choose to subscribe.

How to cancel

  1. Open the app and go to Settings as a parent.
  2. Under Subscription, press Cancel.
You can also cancel from your PayFast account, using the link in your PayFast receipt email, or by emailing info@stickypaper.co.za and we will cancel it for you.
  • You can cancel at any time. There are no cancellation fees.
  • After you cancel, no further payments are taken.
  • You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for (the end of the month or year).

Refunds

  • Cooling-off period: if you change your mind, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of your first payment. This applies to your first monthly or annual payment.
  • After 7 days: we do not give refunds for a period that has already started, including the remainder of a month or year after cancelling. You can still use the Service until the paid period ends.
  • Renewal payments: if you forgot to cancel and were charged for a renewal, email us within 7 days of that charge and, if you have not used the Service in the new period, we will refund it.
  • Mistakes or faults: if you were charged incorrectly (for example twice), or a fault on our side stops you using the Service for a significant time, we will correct the charge or refund the affected period.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act or other South African law.

How to ask for a refund

Email info@stickypaper.co.za from the email address you signed up with and tell us the date of the payment. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method through PayFast, and can take up to 7 to 10 business days to show in your account, depending on your bank.
