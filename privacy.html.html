Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Effective immediately

We built StickyPaper Weekly Planner for busy families. We take your privacy seriously — we collect only what we need to make the app work, and we never sell your data.

1. Who we are

StickyPaper Weekly Planner is operated by StickyPaper, a South African business. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at info@stickypaper.co.za.

2. What information we collect

When you use the app, we collect the following:

  • Google account information — your name, email address and profile photo, provided when you sign in with Google. We use this to identify your account and display your name in the app.
  • Planner data — tasks, habits, cleaning lists, dinner plans, notes, events, and settings that you create inside the app. This is stored securely in Google Firestore under your unique account ID.
  • Google Calendar data — if you choose to connect your Google Calendar, the app reads your calendar events to display them alongside your planner. We do not store your calendar events on our servers — they are fetched directly from Google and displayed in your browser only.
  • People and colour settings — names and colours you add in Settings to colour-code your calendar events. These are stored as part of your planner data.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not collect payment information
  • We do not track your behaviour across other websites
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties
  • We do not write to or modify your Google Calendar

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Sign you in and identify your account
  • Save and sync your planner data across your devices
  • Display your Google Calendar events within the app
  • Remember your app settings and preferences

5. How your data is stored

Your planner data is stored in Google Firestore, a secure cloud database provided by Google. Each user's data is private and protected by security rules — only you can read or write your own data. Google Calendar data is never written to our database; it is fetched live from Google's servers each time you open the app.

6. Google API scopes we request

When you connect your Google Calendar, we request the following permission:

  • calendar.readonly — allows the app to read your Google Calendar events. We use this only to display events in the app. We do not create, edit or delete any calendar events.

Our use of Google Calendar data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data retention

Your planner data is kept for as long as you have an active account. If you wish to delete your data, please contact us at info@stickypaper.co.za and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

StickyPaper Weekly Planner is designed for use by parents and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — for authentication and data storage
  • Google Calendar API — for reading calendar events
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting the app

10. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by signing out and contacting us to delete your account
  • Disconnect Google Calendar access at any time within the app

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
